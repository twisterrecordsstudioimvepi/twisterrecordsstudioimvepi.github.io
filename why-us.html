Why Artists Choose Twister

1. Local Studio, Global Sound

No need to spend on transport to Kampala. Get the same Neumann mics, SSL sound, and Pro Tools workflow right here in Imvepi. Your money stays in the community.

2. We Understand Your Story

Our engineers are from the settlement. We know refugee artists face unique challenges. We offer payment plans, Swahili/Arabic sessions, and late-night bookings.

3. Speed Without Sacrificing Quality

Mix delivered in 48hrs. Mastering in 24hrs. Revisions within 12hrs. We built fast workflows because we know you have deadlines for competitions and radio.

4. Transparent Pricing

No hidden fees. What we quote is what you pay. Student discounts: 20% off with valid ID. Group bookings: record 3 songs, get 1 mix free.
